Da Bologna a Valencia
Once upon a time, three boys came up the Adriatic coast to study at the Alma Mater of Bologna: one from Ancona, two from Pescara. They became friends. Then life scattered them: two ended up in the same software company in Bologna, Cezanne, building an HR portal; the third ran off to Valencia, where he lived at his parents' expense, occupying himself with art and technology.

L’idea del calcetto
Late 2005. In the lunch breaks at Cezanne, Donatello and Gianfranco were hunting for an idea of their own. The favourite: booking five-a-side football pitches online. So they went to interview the only sports-centre owner they knew, Donatello's cousin. No computer at the desk, a paper diary, no intention to change. The football idea died that afternoon.

Nati da remoto
Donatello went looking for a world where computers already existed, and found one experiment in America: 'Ask Mabel', the medical receptionist you could book online. In Europe: nothing. And remember 2006: no iPhone, no apps, no cloud. Two doctors out of three kept their appointments on paper. They called the third friend in Valencia, because there was no AI to ask. He and Gianfranco built the first product together, starting from an open-source project and rebuilding it piece by piece, much of the early work at Gianfranco's house, with a few friends helping for free. So, somewhere between 2005 and 2006, three friends founded a company. Tuotempo was born remote: Donatello's flat in via San Felice in Bologna on one end, Valencia on the other, Skype in between, and a cheap flight every now and then.

Client number one
The idea was not a marketplace, but an online diary each doctor could put on his own website. Except most doctors had no website, and no diary a computer could read. So they went knocking on dentists' doors, starting with the nearest one: Donatello's own dentist, in Bologna. He said yes, and became client number one. One of them, on the viali of Bologna, threw them out so thoroughly that they looked at each other and said: it cannot be this hard. But by then, client number one was already booking online.
